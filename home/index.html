Let’s talk
Who is Coldline for?

Coldline is being built for fresh-produce exporters, cold-store operators, and logistics teams that need to review shipment condition before a handover. The focus is on connecting evidence and helping the quality operator decide what to inspect next.

Can I try it before we talk?

Yes. Use the sample review above, or explore the full demo workspace. You can inspect evidence, prepare a review brief, and work with illustrative shipment records without connecting your own systems.

Does Coldline connect to our temperature devices?

Device and sector data integrations are on the roadmap. The current workspace uses prepared examples, and the server-side Azure AI connector is ready for configuration. We’ll agree the integration scope around your actual workflow.

Does a warning decide whether produce is safe?

A warning points to evidence that needs review. It does not certify food safety or authorize a shipment’s release. Your qualified quality team checks the source records and makes the final decision.
