01MVP terms

Coldline is provided as a B2B software MVP. No customer contract, subscription, regulated service, legal entity or operational guarantee is established by viewing the site or using the demo.

02Sample data, pricing and calculations

Names, people, routes in sample operational records, scores, volumes, and example outcomes are illustrative. Published USD plans define proposed commercial packages; a subscription begins only under an agreed service order. They are not testimonials, actual customer traction, validated forecasts or measured product performance. Arithmetic calculators disclose their assumptions.

03No professional decision service

The condition review supports a qualified operator’s inspection. It does not certify food safety, authorize shipment release, or replace produce-quality and cold-chain handling requirements.

04Availability and data loss

The browser-local demo is supplied as-is for evaluation. Browser storage may be cleared or unavailable. Do not rely on it as a system of record or use confidential or personal operational information.

05Photography credits

Industry photographs are included under their recorded licenses. They show operating context and do not represent customer sites or company assets. Sources, authors, modifications and licenses are listed below.

06Fonts and brand artwork

Inter is hosted locally under its SIL Open Font License. Legacy Space Grotesk and Manrope assets are retained under their accompanying open font licenses. The Coldline SVG mark and wordmark treatment were created for this product. This product does not assert trademark clearance or affiliation with an existing business.

07Before commercial use

Review brand availability, replace fixture data with authorized records, activate and verify the included production identity and data services, validate operating claims and prices, and adopt legal terms suited to the actual business and jurisdiction.

08Photography and attribution
