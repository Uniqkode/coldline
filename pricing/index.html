A few details before we begin.

What does the price cover?+

The stated workspace and processing allowance. Confirm source integration, access, data handling and any additional service use in the onboarding scope.

Can I inspect the workflow first?+

Yes. The public review desk and workspace include prepared, source-linked cases. You can explore them without entering confidential information.

What is required for live analysis?+

An actual compatible model deployment and server-side credentials, authorized input sources and appropriate organizational access controls. The Azure connector is implemented; deployment configuration and sector integrations are separate milestones.

Are customer counts implied by these plans?+

No. These are the commercial package definitions. Actual users, throughput and measured outcomes are published only when supported by verified records.
