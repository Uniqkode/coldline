Put the pallet image in its shipment context

FIELD NOTE

Evidence.
Ownership.
A practical next step.

Explore the platform

AI uses produce images and temperature histories to identify shipments at risk.

Start with the input the team already has.

A shipment’s condition can change between collection and delivery. Images, door events and temperature histories must be reviewed repeatedly across many active loads.

For produce exporters, cold stores, and logistics teams. Coldline reviews pallet photos alongside temperature records to show which shipments need cold-chain attention.

Make the review practical.

  1. Use the product’s actual handling range rather than a universal temperature threshold.
  2. Link each pallet image to the correct checkpoint and capture time.
  3. A condition warning should name the evidence, not assert a cause it cannot prove.
  4. Keep the quality operator’s final decision beside the warning.

One concrete example.

A carrier log shows a temperature excursion while the pallet image sits in a separate quality folder.

The prepared report connects the 35-minute excursion with the pallet that needs an inspection.

Ask the quality operator to inspect the pallet and record the result.

Keep the decision with the responsible person.

An operator inspects, contacts the carrier and records the action. The warning supports an inspection. It does not certify food safety or replace a qualified quality decision.

The product’s sample workspace lets you follow that chain: source, finding, review and retained action. The example is prepared for demonstration and does not claim measured customer performance.
